Harbor & Vine needed a launch party that felt as polished as their new product, on a rooftop with a strict two-hour window.

The rooftop had a freight lift only, and the bar had to be set up during the final rehearsal of the presentation.

We created six branded cocktails, set up two copper bars in under forty minutes and passed bites from our catering team throughout.

180 guests served, no queues, and three new corporate bookings from guests on the night.
