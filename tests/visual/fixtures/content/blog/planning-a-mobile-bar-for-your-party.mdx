A mobile bar turns any party into an event. Here is how to plan one, from guest count to glassware.

> One bartender for every fifty guests means nobody waits more than a minute for a drink.

![A bartender shaking a cocktail at a copper mobile bar cart on a patio at dusk](/vr/post-body-planning-a-mobile-bar.jpg)

Our bar team plans every detail, but these are the decisions we’ll ask you to make.

- Your guest count and timings
- Full bar, beer and wine, or cocktails only
- Signature drinks and a zero-proof option
- Where the bar will stand and power access
- Who supplies the spirits

Tell us about your party and we’ll send a bar plan and quote within one business day.
