Our **Private Chef Dinners** bring a restaurant kitchen to your home for one evening. Every menu is written for your table and cooked from ingredients we bought that morning. We take care of:

- **Menu Design:** A tasting menu of four to nine courses, written around the season, the occasion and your guests.
- **Sourcing:** Seafood from Shem Creek, vegetables from Johns Island farms and meat from Lowcountry producers we know by name.
- **Table Service:** A chef and a dedicated server who plate, pour and clear so you never leave the table.
- **Clean-Up:** Every pot, plate and glass washed and your kitchen left spotless.

It starts with a call about the occasion and your guests’ tastes and allergies. We send a draft menu, cook a tasting if you’d like one, and arrive three hours before your first guest.

Perfect for anniversaries, birthdays, holiday dinners, vacation rentals and anyone who would rather host than cook.
